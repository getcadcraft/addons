---
title: Tagged outlines
summary: Draw closed outlines that carry a tag, read their area and perimeter, and export them as CSV.
section: tasks
order: 10
keywords: outline, tag, area, perimeter, csv
---

Type {{cmd:OUTLINE}} (or click {{ui:Outline}} in the Outlines group), click the corners, press Enter to close the
outline, then type its tag. To turn a closed polyline you already have into an outline, use {{cmd:OUTLINEFROM}}.

Drag a corner's grip to reshape the outline. Select one to change its tag in the Properties panel, where its area and
perimeter are shown too.

{{cmd:EXPORT}} lists **Outlines (CSV)**: one row per outline with its tag, area, perimeter, number of corners and layer.

:::tip
A drawing with outlines opens in any CAD program as ordinary block references, and the tags travel with them.
:::
